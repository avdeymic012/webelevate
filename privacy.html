Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and who helps us handle it. Last updated November 13, 2026.

Who we are

WebElevate is a small studio that designs and builds websites for businesses. This policy covers this website, webelevateagency.com, and the inquiries and projects that come through it. You can reach us at michael@webelevateagency.com.

What we collect and why

When you send us a message

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, and message. It also lets you share a phone number (optional) and tick the services you're interested in. We use this only to reply to your inquiry, answer your questions, and put together a quote or plan if you'd like one.

If you become a client

To do the work and run the project, we keep the details you give us: contact and business information, the content and materials for your site, what we agreed on, and our invoices and signed agreements.

When you just browse

We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

Cookies

This website doesn't set cookies or store information in your browser. If that ever changes, we'll update this page first.

Who helps us handle your information

We use a few outside services to run the website and the business. Each one handles only what it needs to do its job:

  • Web3Forms receives what you type into our contact form and delivers it to us.
  • Google Workspace hosts our business email, where your inquiry arrives and where we reply. We also use Google Sheets to keep intake details for clients.
  • Vercel hosts this website. To send you our pages, a host like Vercel may process technical information such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts you see on our pages. When a page loads, your browser contacts Google to download them, which shares your IP address and basic browser details with Google.
  • Stripe processes payments for clients.
  • SignWell handles electronic signatures on agreements.
  • Todoist is where we keep our project and task notes for client work.

Some pages link to other sites, such as Trustpilot and LinkedIn. Once you click through, that site's own privacy policy applies.

We don't sell your information

We don't sell personal information. We share it only with the services listed above, so we can reply to you and do the work.

How long we keep it

We retain personal information only as long as needed to respond to inquiries, provide our services, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Email michael@webelevateagency.com and tell us what you'd like. If we're required to keep certain records, such as tax or contract records, we'll let you know what we can and can't delete.

Security

We choose established services to handle your information, but no method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so we can't promise absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions

Write to us at michael@webelevateagency.com and we'll help.
