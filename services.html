How working together works

The practical details, up front, so there are no surprises.

A short discovery call, plus your existing branding, text, and photography. If you don't have those yet, professional copywriting, photography, and substantial content creation can be quoted separately.

Your site is custom-built, so changes go through us. Minor edits fit within a care plan's monthly allowance, and other work is billed at $75/hour with your approval.

Care plan terms

Each plan includes a monthly allowance for minor content updates, and unused time doesn't roll over. Additional work outside the allowance is billed separately at $75/hour, subject to your approval.

Major redesigns, new pages, and advanced functionality aren't included. Care plans also don't include comprehensive ongoing SEO campaigns or guaranteed search rankings, and they don't promise unlimited edits, unlimited support, or guaranteed uptime.

Hosting, domains, and third-party subscriptions are billed separately unless your agreement says otherwise. Updates and backups depend on your website's hosting platform and technical configuration. Support response times and specific responsibilities are defined in your service agreement.
